The project

01 / PROJECT

comprarcarne.es is an online butcher’s from Málaga that delivers fresh meat — vacuum-packed and chilled — to homes across mainland Spain. The idea: a TV spot that turns the purchase itself into the story, from the first tap on the phone to the first bite, with a family as the cast. Client work by AI Creative Lead, directed by César Cabrera.

The challenge

02 / PROBLEM

Kids, food and a real product: three of the hardest things to shoot.

A real client with a real product. The spot had to show the brand’s own delivery box — colour, logo and printed copy — make meat look appetizing on screen and carry a family through a house where the camera never stops, in a single TV slot.

  • The client’s real box, exact, revealed only when the doorbell rings.
  • Food that makes you hungry: golden crust, sizzle, knife and fork.
  • Kids of clearly different ages and faces — brothers, not twins.

Creative direction

03 / DECISION

Spanish colour at full volume and a camera that never stops. The story is a countdown — order, wait, doorbell, box, pan, table — and the product enters for real at the exact moment the doorbell rings.

04 / CAST

The family was designed before production and dressed in the palette of the set: Mum in mustard, the boys in blue, green and yellow — clearly different ages, heights and faces. Brothers, not twins.

The box is the hero.

05 / PRODUCT

Before the doorbell, the box only exists as a photo on a phone and a child’s crayon drawing. When the door opens it arrives for real — the client’s own box, exact colour, logo and printed copy — and from there the story is what’s inside: vacuum-packed cuts that end up seared on the pan and sliced on the table.

Can an AI commercial show a brand’s real product?

Yes. Here the client’s real delivery box — colour, logo and printed copy — was reproduced from reference images and only revealed when the doorbell rings.

Can food look appetizing in an AI-generated ad?

Yes, when it is directed like a food shoot: golden crust and sizzle on the pan, real knife-and-fork use, and every plate reviewed shot by shot.

03

Can an AI commercial feature children?

Yes. The children are AI-generated characters — no real children are filmed — and the family is designed before production starts.
